## Current project constraints

The user's instructions supersede the archived kit:

- Do not use or integrate with Lovable.
- Do not use or integrate with LearnWorlds.
- Any references to those services in the original archive are obsolete and must not become build requirements or setup guidance.
- Exclude visitor registration, login, logout and password recovery.
- Exclude user accounts, profiles and account management.
- Exclude memberships, community roles, subscriptions and payments.
- Exclude member-only content and restricted downloads. Published content and downloads are public.
- Exclude all CMS functionality, including Sanity Studio.
- Exclude administrator dashboards and browser-based content editing.
- Exclude database infrastructure for these features.
- Maintain editorial content in repository files, with updates published through the build/deployment workflow.

## Intent and direction

A knowledge house for practical thinking in technology-intensive B2B businesses. Guiding phrase: “mystery at the entrance, clarity when exploring.” It is an editorial knowledge space with participation, rather than a personal profile.

## Reference requirements from the kit

- Lowercase identity: `tolpagorni`, `tolpagorni thoughts`, `magnus meets`; legal footer name `tolpagorni thoughts AB`.
- Navigation: knowledge, research, magnus meets, participate, about; emblem links home.
- Homepage order: entrance, Pulse, latest thought, perspective, down the rabbit hole, magnus meets, unanswered question, library, participate.
- Midnight blue, warm white, green and glacial blue; no purple/violet. Calm italic headings, serif editorial body, typewriter labels.
- Use supplied mountain line artwork and emblem. Mountain is atmosphere; avoid climbing/summit metaphors. Client-owned photos are supplied.
- Restrained aurora and question animations, reduced-motion fallbacks, off-screen animation pause, WCAG 2.2 AA, no page overflow at 360px.
- English; Vimeo with captions. Editorial content is maintained in repository files.
- Pulse derives from real dates: fortnightly thoughts, monthly perspectives, quarterly research updates, annual August publication. No decorative progress percentages.
- Knowledge can progress from thought to investigation to publication to library, without requiring that sequence.
- File-based structured content, relationships, archives, search, filters, public downloads and investigation milestones. The Pulse is generated from content dates during builds.
- Participation must work without visitor accounts, an administrator dashboard or a database-backed moderation queue; its delivery workflow remains to be selected.
- Mockup examples are placeholders, not approved launch content.
