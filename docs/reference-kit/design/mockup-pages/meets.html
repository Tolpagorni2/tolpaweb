What should never be replatformed?

A conversation with a CTO in industrial technology about what a platform shift quietly removes, and how to notice it in time.

observationsselected, short
  1. The old system knew things nobody had written down, such as which customers always pay late and why.
  2. The best question to ask before a migration is what people do around the system, not in it.
  3. Moving fast is fine for the parts that change every week. It is dangerous for the parts that change every ten years.
