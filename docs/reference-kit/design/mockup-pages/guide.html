A direction, not a drawing to copy

These pages show the feeling tolpagorni thoughts is looking for. The designer and developer are free to change how it looks and works, within a few fixed points.

fixed and openwhat can change

fixed

  • Nordic, calm, a little mysterious, never corporate. Mystery at the entrance, clarity when exploring.
  • The house in front, not one person. Names always in lowercase.
  • The five menu items, the homepage parts and the publishing rhythm.
  • The mountain only as atmosphere, with the Speed Layers lines and the emblem.
  • Northern lights in green and glacial blue, never purple.
  • Soft, quiet typography. Nothing loud.
  • Progress shown with real dates and milestones.
  • tolpagorni thoughts publishes without a developer. English only. Video on Vimeo.

open to the designer and developer

  • Layout, spacing and the look of each part.
  • Typefaces and exact colour values, in the same spirit.
  • How the northern lights, the pulse and the moving questions are drawn and animated.
  • Page templates, components, forms and small interactions.
  • The tech stack, as long as publishing stays simple and the code is owned by tolpagorni thoughts AB.
  • Ideas that go further. Show a sketch first.
