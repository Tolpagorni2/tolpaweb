Knowledge travels like a song

Knowledge is not an isolated object. It lives, changes, and takes new forms as it moves between people, teams, organizations, and situations. The same song can be performed by different musicians, with different instruments, in different settings. It remains recognizable, but the sound changes.

That is why knowledge needs to be designed for travel. When knowledge becomes a model, it becomes easier to carry, and easier to detach from the situation where it was created.
