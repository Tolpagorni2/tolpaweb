# tolpagorni thoughts
## Website concept & structure brief
Version 0.7 | October 2026

> This is the client's brief, kept as written. Where later decisions refine it (naming in lowercase, softer type, no purple), see `02-decisions.md`. Those decisions win where the two differ.

### 1. Vision and purpose

**Thoughts need room to breathe.**

tolpagorni thoughts is a living space for practical thinking, exploration, and knowledge development in technology-intensive businesses.

The site brings together thoughts, applied research, practical resources, conversations, cultural perspectives, and contributions from others.

It should not feel like a traditional corporate website, research institution, or online journal.

Instead, it should feel like entering an interesting place. A place where there is always something developing, something to discover, and occasionally something unexpected.

The website should combine three qualities:

- **Atmosphere:** Nordic nature, the Tolpagorni mountain, northern lights, space, and a slightly mysterious character.
- **Substance:** Practical knowledge about products, technology, systems, services, organizations, and how they evolve.
- **Participation:** Opportunities to explore, contribute, question, review, and develop knowledge together.

The guiding design principle is **mystery at the entrance, clarity when exploring**.

---

### 2. Overall website structure

The website has two interconnected layers.

**Layer 1: The atmospheric entrance**

A distinctive landing experience with the mountain landscape, northern lights, typography, and an invitation to explore.

The entrance should create curiosity while providing immediate access to the site's main areas and current activities.

**Layer 2: The knowledge space**

The functional website, containing organized knowledge, research, conversations, perspectives, and opportunities to participate.

The navigation remains recognizable, while content changes according to a defined publishing rhythm.

#### Primary navigation

| Section | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **knowledge** | The home for thoughts, perspectives, established knowledge, publications, and resources |
| **research** | Ongoing investigations, questions, research progress, and deeper explorations |
| **magnus meets** | Video conversations with interesting people about products, technology, and different perspectives |
| **participate** | Ways to contribute, challenge, review, and collaborate |
| **about** | The purpose, philosophy, people, and development of tolpagorni thoughts |

The logo always returns to the homepage.

The homepage also provides direct editorial entry points, such as "A Perspective", "Down the Rabbit Hole", and "The Unanswered Question".

These are recognizable concepts within the site rather than additional top-level navigation items.

### 3. Homepage architecture

The homepage is the living center of tolpagorni thoughts.

It should have a relatively stable layout while displaying changing content, active investigations, and upcoming milestones.

#### Section A: The entrance

A full-screen or near-full-screen atmospheric opening.

Elements:
- tolpagorni mountain emblem
- tolpagorni thoughts identity
- Northern-lights animation in the background
- Animated topographical mountain lines based on the Speed Layers artwork
- Main statement: "thoughts need room to breathe"
- Brief explanation of the purpose
- An "enter" invitation
- A few direct routes into the website

The mountain landscape and northern lights should create the impression of entering a place rather than visiting a conventional homepage.

The "enter" action leads smoothly into the content rather than requiring a separate loading or splash screen.

#### Section B: The Pulse

A permanent, recognizable feature showing the rhythm of the website.

The Pulse presents the latest content, ongoing progress, and upcoming significant contributions.

It should be clear what has recently happened, what is currently developing, and what comes next.

#### Section C: The latest thought

The most recent fortnightly thought, prominently displayed.

A relatively short reflection, practical observation, or provocative question.

Visitors can open the full thought and browse previous thoughts.

#### Section D: A Perspective

A monthly perspective from an artist, thinker, designer, scientist, author, or another interesting source.

This space can be somewhat unexpected and occasionally outside the normal domain of product development.

Its purpose is to stimulate curiosity and bring different ways of seeing familiar questions.

#### Section E: Down the Rabbit Hole

An invitation to explore something more deeply.

Featuring one ongoing investigation or important question, with clear access to its background, progress, supporting materials, and emerging findings.

#### Section F: Magnus Meets

The latest video conversation, with a short introduction.

Visitors can open the episode, browse earlier conversations, or explore related topics.

#### Section G: The Unanswered Question

A prominently displayed open question.

This might be related to an ongoing investigation or simply something worth thinking about.

Visitors should be able to contribute a perspective or explore what others have contributed.

#### Section H: From the Library

A selection of established resources, frameworks, tools, or publications.

This connects the changing homepage to the more permanent knowledge base.

#### Section I: Participate

An invitation to take part.

This could highlight a specific investigation seeking input, a publication open for review, or an opportunity to propose an idea.

### 4. The Pulse: the heartbeat of the website

tolpagorni thoughts follows a defined editorial rhythm.

Each rhythm has a different purpose.

| Rhythm | Defined contribution | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Fortnightly** | A new Thought | Short, practical, reflective |
| **Monthly** | A Perspective | Unexpected, curious, inspiring |
| **Quarterly** | A substantial exploration update | Deeper, evidence-based, applied |
| **Yearly, in August** | A major publication | Developed, reusable knowledge |

**Fortnightly: A Thought**

Every two weeks, publish a new reflection or observation, typically 300–500 words.

It should contain a recognizable thought, not simply an announcement or update for the sake of activity.

**Monthly: A Perspective**

Introduce one selected perspective, person, artistic work, book, film, website, or other source of inspiration.

Include a short explanation of why it is interesting.

**Quarterly: A deeper exploration**

Four times per year, present significant progress from an investigation or knowledge development initiative.

This could include working models, field observations, new evidence, emerging conclusions, or new resources.

**Annually: The August Publication**

Every August, release one substantial new contribution.

The format may change from year to year: a booklet, framework, research synthesis, practical guide, or collaboratively developed piece of knowledge.

The August publication becomes a recurring landmark in the life of tolpagorni thoughts.

#### Visualizing the pulse

The publishing rhythms should be subtly reflected in the website design.

Possible features include:
- A recurring pulse line or visual rhythm
- Dates of the latest updates
- Clear publication milestones
- Progress updates for active investigations
- A visible path toward the annual August publication
- Subtle seasonal variations inspired by Nordic light

Progress should represent actual developments and milestones, not decorative percentages.

The visual system should feel alive without becoming restless.

### 5. The knowledge architecture

There are three different states of knowledge on the site.

**Emerging thoughts**

Short observations, questions, perspectives, and hypotheses. Some will remain as individual thoughts.

**Developing knowledge**

Ideas being investigated, tested, compared, challenged, and refined through ongoing work.

This is principally represented by "Down the Rabbit Hole".

**Established knowledge**

Developed and published resources that can be used, shared, referenced, and revisited.

This is primarily represented by the Library.

An idea may develop through these states:

Thought → Investigation → Publication → Library

This is a possible development path, not a mandatory process.

#### The Library

The Library should be carefully organized and relatively stable.

It contains:
- Publications and booklets
- Frameworks and models
- Practical tools and templates
- Research papers and reports
- Curated reference material
- Knowledge organized into thematic groups

Resources should be searchable and connected to relevant research, thoughts, and conversations.

#### Down the Rabbit Hole

This is the primary space for deeper investigation.

Each investigation should have a dedicated page containing:

1. The central question or challenge
2. Background and practical relevance
3. Current hypotheses and perspectives
4. Activities and work in progress
5. Findings and supporting material
6. Latest progress and next steps
7. Opportunities to contribute
8. Related resources and publications

Visitors should be able to understand both what is being explored and how the understanding is developing.

### 6. Magnus Meets

Magnus Meets is a recurring series of recorded conversations.

The ambition is not to produce polished, formal interviews but to explore interesting questions, products, experiences, and perspectives with interesting people.

Each episode should have:

- Video
- Guest and topic introduction
- Central question or theme
- Short summary or selected observations
- Related resources and links
- Connections to other content on the site

Episodes should be browsable by topic, person, and date.

Magnus Meets should feel personal and conversational while being fully integrated into the wider knowledge space.

---

### 7. Participation and community

tolpagorni thoughts should gradually become a place where people contribute to knowledge development, not just consume content.

Participation should be connected to meaningful activities.

Initial participation options:
- Suggest a thought or unanswered question
- Recommend a perspective
- Contribute practical experiences
- Join a specific investigation
- Review a developing publication
- Suggest a guest for Magnus Meets

Over time, a more structured contributor model may emerge, with roles such as Member, Contributor, Reviewer, and Fellow.

An open discussion forum is not a launch requirement.

The priority is meaningful contributions around identifiable questions, investigations, and knowledge.

### 8. Visual identity and interaction

The current homepage mockup is the primary visual direction.

**Identity**

The Tolpagorni mountain, its emblem, topographical line drawings, and the existing northern-lights film create the visual foundation.

The mountain lines can animate into view as the page opens. The northern lights move subtly behind them.

**Color**

Dark midnight blue, warm white, muted glacial colors, and a restrained green accent.

**Typography**

A distinctive combination of expressive modern typography for the large statements and readable editorial typography for longer content.

**Interaction**

Restrained animation, smooth transitions, subtle movements, and simple navigation.

The site should feel contemporary and technologically sophisticated without being overly experimental or difficult to use.

**Atmosphere**

Nordic, thoughtful, intriguing, independent, and slightly unconventional.

Not overly corporate, not overly artistic, and not excessively minimal.

**Design reference**

Formafantasma is the strongest current reference for the balance between visual restraint, serious content, and distinctive identity.

Cambio, by Formafantasma, is a useful reference for presenting deeper investigations and associated material.

### 9. Functional requirements

The website should be built around a straightforward content management system, allowing frequent updates without requiring a developer.

The content structure should support distinct content types:

- Thought
- Perspective
- Investigation
- Research update
- Question
- Magnus Meets episode
- Resource
- Publication

Content should be connected through subjects, tags, and relationships.

Essential capabilities:

- Easily publish and schedule content
- Maintain an automatic archive
- Display the current Pulse
- Track investigation milestones and progress
- Associate related content across sections
- Embed video
- Make resources downloadable
- Support search and filtering
- Accept structured contributions
- Work well on mobile devices
- Remain accessible and performant

Animations should respect reduced-motion preferences and never interfere with content readability.

- Research collaborations

**Later possibilities**

- Personalized interests and subscriptions
- Thematic collections
- Interactive tools and models
- More sophisticated community functions
- Special digital experiences for annual publications

### 11. The intended character

tolpagorni thoughts should be a place people enjoy returning to because something meaningful is always developing.

A place for practical thinkers who are curious about products, technology, systems, and how people make things happen.

A place where different perspectives are welcome, unfinished thoughts can be shared, and useful knowledge can grow through conversation, experience, and investigation.

A little mysterious at first.

Quite practical once you enter.

And always with room to breathe.
